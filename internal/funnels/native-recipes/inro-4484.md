## Applicability
- An existing Live invitation directs viewers to ordinary DMs, where they choose between two options.

## Prerequisites
- The coach places the invitation; prepare one preference question and both named destinations.

## Exclusions
- Do not choose when Live comments must trigger automation without a new DM. Clarify unknown preferences.
- Do not configure when required lifecycle operations are unavailable on the actual host.

End this campaign on refusal. Do not restart from a keyword in the refusal.
